Import a Florida or Arizona snapshot

Choose your state in REC, open the official portal in Safari, and sign in there. Navigate to the remaining-allotment or order page, take a screenshot, return to Weed Authority, and choose Import Screenshot. Review the value, unit, and route or category, then confirm before saving.

No value was found

The importer accepts only supported Florida and Arizona labels with an explicit unit. Make sure the label and value are visible in the screenshot. If the result is missing or ambiguous, do not guess; check the portal and enter the verified value manually.

Why does my snapshot say stale?

A confirmed snapshot is marked stale after 24 hours or invalidated when you record a new purchase. Recheck the official portal before relying on it. The state registry and licensed dispensary are always authoritative.

Other states

Screenshot import is currently limited to supported Florida and Arizona screens. Other state sections provide official source links and private manual tracking; they do not claim a live state balance.

Portal privacy

Weed Authority never receives your portal username, password, MFA code, cookies, or Safari session. It does not log in for you, automate portal navigation, inject scripts, or read portal page content. A selected screenshot is read on the device and discarded by the app after import; your original remains in Photos until you delete it there.

Local data

Open Account in the app to delete your local rec profile, confirmed Keychain snapshots, saved products, saved retailers, and purchase ledger entries. Delete any original portal screenshots separately from Photos.

Ads and privacy choices

Google AdMob banners appear on non-REC screens only and do not receive REC or health values. Open Account → Ad choices to review available consent and privacy controls.

Retailer verification

Always verify license details with the state regulator before ordering or traveling to a retailer.

Report a problem safely

Send the state, app version, affected label, and a description of the issue. Do not email portal screenshots, card numbers, patient identifiers, passwords, MFA codes, or other medical information.
