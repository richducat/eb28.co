Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem with Limitless Credit GPS? Email support@eb28.co and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app (or clearing site data in your browser) removes it.

Why did my plan reset?

Plans are saved per device and per browser. If you cleared browser data or reinstalled the app, the quiz starts fresh.

Does the app show my real credit score?

No. Limitless Credit GPS never pulls your credit. It provides educational, directional estimates based on the answers you enter.
