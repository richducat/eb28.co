How the SYNC economy works

Greeting a Sync and playing Dig both cost SYNC, and the reward is random — sometimes more than you spent, often nothing at all. The exact odds are published in the app before you spend. You also earn SYNC in ways that don't cost anything: a 20 SYNC welcome airdrop, up to 100 SYNC a week from check-in streaks, invites, staked crew, and step-goal boosts. SyncStep is a game, not an income source — play with what you're happy to spend on entertainment.

SYNC is a game currency, not money. You can top up by buying a SYNC pack with SOL from the Wallet tab (from 0.005 SOL). SYNC is spent inside SyncStep only — it cannot be cashed out, withdrawn, sold back or transferred, and purchases are final. “Earn” here means earning SYNC to play with, never income.
