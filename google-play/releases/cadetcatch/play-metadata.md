# CadetCatch Google Play Metadata

## Store Listing Draft

App name: CadetCatch

Short description:
Find possible event-photo matches for your cadet from CadetCatch photo collections.

Full description:
CadetCatch helps parents and families review event photos for possible matches of their cadet. Add one clear photo of your cadet, choose a match range, and CadetCatch checks the photo collections that have been uploaded and indexed by the CadetCatch team.

The app returns possible matches for you to review. High match range is strict, Medium looks a little wider for different angles, and Low can find tougher side-profile photos but may include more lookalikes.

CadetCatch is designed for family photo review. It does not guarantee identity and it does not search photos that have not been uploaded and indexed by the CadetCatch team.

## Parent-Facing Notes

- Say "possible matches", not guaranteed matches.
- Say "uploaded and indexed event photos", not "the whole internet" or "every photo".
- Keep developer terms like API, Qdrant, R2, embeddings, and vector search out of store copy.
- Keep search, account access, family/cadet invites, and photo save/open behavior aligned with the iOS app.
