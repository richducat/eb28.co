Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Limitless Credit GPS works without an account and does not collect personal information. Quiz answers, credit profile estimates, saved simulations, and plan progress are stored locally on the user's device and never leave it.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not require registration, does not ask for a Social Security number, does not pull credit reports, and does not transmit user-entered answers, balances, or simulation results to any server. All app data is stored locally on the device using standard browser or iOS local storage.

Advertising and Tracking

Limitless Credit GPS contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking of individual users.

Fonts and External Links

The app loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which involves a standard network request to Google's font servers (Google may receive the requesting IP address as part of serving the file). Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. If a user chooses to open an external website from the app, that site loads in the browser and has its own privacy practices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer personal information. There is nothing collected to share.

Deletion

Deleting the app from an iPhone removes all locally stored app data. On the web, clearing the browser's site data for eb28.co removes all stored answers and simulations.

Not Financial or Legal Advice

Limitless Credit GPS provides educational estimates based on common credit scoring factors. It does not guarantee score changes, credit approvals, account removals, or financial outcomes, and it does not provide financial, credit repair, or legal advice.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@eb28.co.
