# CadetCatch Parent Landing Page Copy

## Copy Brief

- **Copy type:** App download landing page
- **Product:** CadetCatch for iPhone
- **Target audience:** One Coast Guard Academy parent or guardian who checks new public photo galleries for one familiar face
- **Key transformation:** Move from opening every photo and wondering what they missed to reviewing a shorter list of possible matches and saving the photos they recognize
- **Tone:** Warm, direct, protective, and honest
- **Constraints:** One primary App Store action; no guaranteed identification; no fabricated ratings, testimonials, user counts, or time-saved claims

## Version A: PAS

**Why this framework:** The parent already knows the problem. The strongest opening names the exact gallery-search ritual, then shows how CadetCatch makes the review more focused without claiming certainty.

### Hero

- **Kicker:** For Coast Guard Academy parents
- **Headline:** Find the face you know by heart.
- **Body:** A new gallery goes up. You zoom into group shots and still wonder if you missed them. CadetCatch narrows the photos worth a closer look. You decide which ones are really your cadet.
- **CTA:** Download CadetCatch free
- **Friction reducer:** iPhone. Free to download. Start with one clear photo.

### Parent problem

- **Headline:** A gallery drops. You start looking for one familiar face.
**Body:** You zoom into group shot after group shot. The haircuts look alike. The uniforms are the same. You reach the end and still wonder if you skipped the photo you wanted.

You know your cadet’s posture, their glasses, and the way they stand in a crowd. CadetCatch gets you closer to the photos where that knowledge matters.

### Solution

- **Headline:** From “Did I miss them?” to photos worth reviewing.
**Body:** Start with the face you know best. Choose the galleries you already follow. Review possible matches with a parent’s eye and save what you recognize.

### Trust

- **Headline:** Your family photo stays your family business.
**Body:** CadetCatch helps you review. It does not decide who anyone is. You choose the photo pages, inspect every suggestion, and keep only what you recognize.

### Final close

- **Headline:** When the next gallery drops, start with CadetCatch.
- **Body:** Add one clear photo now, so your next search starts with a familiar face.
- **CTA:** Download CadetCatch free

## Version B: BAB

**Why this framework:** This direction leads with the emotional change from uncertainty to recognition. It is warmer and more optimistic, but it spends less time validating the frustration that brought the parent to the page.

### Hero

- **Kicker:** Built for the parent waiting for a glimpse
- **Headline:** From “Did I miss them?” to “There you are.”
- **Body:** CadetCatch helps turn a crowded Academy gallery into a shorter list of photos for you to review. You recognize the face. You save the moment.
- **CTA:** Download CadetCatch free
- **Friction reducer:** Free for iPhone. Possible matches are always reviewed by you.

### Before

- **Headline:** Every new photo drop starts the same way.
**Body:** Open the album. Zoom into the group shots. Scan every row. Reach the last photo and wonder whether your cadet was there after all.

### After

- **Headline:** Start with the photos most worth your attention.
**Body:** See possible matches together, inspect each one yourself, and keep the photos that look like the person you know better than anyone.

### Bridge

- **Headline:** One familiar photo gives you a better place to begin.
**Body:** Add your cadet to a private roster, choose the public photo pages you follow, and review the suggestions CadetCatch returns.

### Trust

- **Headline:** You remain the person who decides.
**Body:** CadetCatch can narrow a gallery. It cannot guarantee a match or make an identity decision for your family.

### Final close

- **Headline:** Keep the moments you were hoping to find.
- **Body:** Download CadetCatch free and set up your private roster before the next gallery arrives.
- **CTA:** Download CadetCatch free

## Recommendation

Use Version A first. The parent is problem-aware, and the gallery-search ritual is specific enough to create immediate recognition without invented proof. Version B is the strongest alternate treatment for a future measured test. The live page uses Version A for the conversion arc and borrows “There you are” from Version B as the visual outcome line.
