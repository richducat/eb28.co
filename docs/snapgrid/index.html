More ways to make every run yours

Remix Kits

Choose a puzzle image and short sound cues with Apple’s system pickers. Your kit stays in the app’s local storage and can be changed or reset at any time.

Photo Reveal

Cleared rows and columns reveal the active custom image tile by tile. Empty the full board for a Clean Sweep score bonus.

Power and competition

Earn tokens, use tactical power-ups, chase Game Center leaderboards, or enter an optional 90-second two-player Duel Sprint.

Free to play, with clear choices

Snapgrid Remix includes advertising for free players. Premium Monthly is an optional auto-renewable subscription for ad-free play and the premium neon board theme. Optional 1,000-token and 10,000-token packs are consumable in-app purchases used only inside Snapgrid Remix. Prices are shown by Apple before purchase. No app account is required.
