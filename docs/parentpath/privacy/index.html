Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

ParentPath collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no login, and no server. Your name, case number, county, course progress, quiz scores, practice conversations, and completion certificate are stored only on your own device.

Information we collect

None. ParentPath does not require registration, does not transmit what you type to any server, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The developer cannot see your name, your case, your answers, or your progress.

Information stored on your device

So the course can remember where you left off, the app stores the following locally using standard iOS storage: the name, optional case number, optional county and optional email you enter; which lessons you have read; time spent reading each module; quiz scores; your app settings; and your completion certificate if you earn one. None of it leaves your device unless you choose to share your certificate or progress export through the iOS share sheet.

Practice Lab conversations

The Practice Lab simulates text messages with a co-parent. Those conversations are fictional exercises. They are not sent to anyone, are not retained after you leave a scenario, and are not transmitted off the device. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, coaching responses are generated by Apple's on-device model — that processing also happens entirely on your iPhone.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere, and permission is requested only at the moment you enable the reminder.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all my data removes everything the app has stored and returns it to a first-launch state. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Children

ParentPath is intended for adults involved in a divorce or parenting case. It is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.

Payment

ParentPath is a paid app purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the purchase; the developer never receives your payment details. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Course approval

ParentPath is an educational product covering the topic areas of Florida's Parent Education and Family Stabilization Course (Fla. Stat. § 61.21). It is not currently an approved course provider in any jurisdiction. Confirm accepted providers with the clerk of court in your circuit before relying on this course to satisfy a court requirement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised policy posted at this address.
