Find My Person

Privacy Choices

Manage account deletion and prepare privacy requests without sharing passwords or private case evidence.

Delete your account

Sign in to the app, select Delete account from the command center, and confirm permanent deletion. The app deletes the account and associated service data, except information that must be retained by law.

Request access or correction

Email support@eb28.co

Send the email address associated with the account and the type of request. Do not send a password, one-time code, government identifier, child photograph, or private evidence by email.

Face Match consent

Guardian consent can be enabled or removed for each protected profile. Removing consent clears the selected comparison photos from the active app workspace. Face photos are not uploaded to the service.

Tracking and advertising

The current app does not use cross-app advertising tracking and does not sell personal information.
