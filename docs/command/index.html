The problem

You don't need another inbox. You need to know what matters.

If you run several projects or businesses at once, the work isn't the hard part — the tracking is. Who's waiting on you. What you promised, and when. What quietly broke while you were heads-down on something else. Every tool you add is one more place to check. Command inverts that: it checks everything, and interrupts you exactly once a day with what's actually urgent — in a text message, in plain English, with the reply options already numbered.

How it works

Five steps. One text.

01

Watch

Command monitors your Gmail, Apple Mail, calendar, Stripe/CRM revenue, and messages — on your own machine.

02

Brief

Every morning: one text with your top 3 moves — who's waiting, what's due, what broke.

03

Reply

Text back a number. That's the whole interface. No app to open, no dashboard to check.

04

Approve

Real tasks get proposed as real work. One tap to approve — nothing ever runs without it.

05

Done

AI agents execute on your machine, report back what happened, and the loop closes.

What you get

A chief of staff that never loses the thread.

The morning brief

One text. The top 3 moves.

Not a digest of everything — a judgment call on what matters. Who's waiting on a reply and what they asked. Promises you made, with their deadlines. What broke overnight and how to fix it.

Follow-through

It escalates what you ignore.

Snooze something and it comes back — louder. Deadlines you're about to blow get flagged before they're blown. And every Sunday you get a plain-English summary of the week: what moved, what stalled, what's coming.

Hermes chat

An AI that proposes real work.

Inside the app, Hermes isn't a chatbot that just talks about your business — it can propose actual tasks: draft that reply, chase that invoice, fix that broken page. You see exactly what it wants to do before anything happens.

Agent execution

One tap, and it's actually done.

Approve a task and AI agents (Claude, Codex) execute it on your own machine — with hard rails: nothing runs unapproved, no spending, ever, and everything is reported back to you when it's finished.

Why this exists

Built by someone who was drowning.

Questions

The honest answers.

Where does my data live?

On your machines. Command runs against your own accounts on your own hardware, and the AI calls use your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. Your email, revenue, and messages are not pooled into someone else's cloud.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac and an iPhone, plus your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. Command connects to Gmail, Apple Mail, your calendar, and Stripe or your CRM during onboarding.

How does onboarding work?

Personally, with the founder. Every design partner gets set up one-on-one — your accounts connected, your brief tuned to how you actually work. That's the point of keeping the group small.

Can the AI do things without asking me?

No. Nothing executes without your explicit approval, and spending money is off-limits entirely. Every approved task reports back exactly what it did.

Is this proven, or am I the test?

Both, honestly. The founder runs 50+ projects with Command every day — the morning brief, reply-by-text, escalation, and agent execution all work today, for him. Design partners are how it gets battle-tested beyond one person's setup. That's why the price is $29 and the seat count is small.

Who is this for?

Founders and solo operators juggling multiple projects or businesses — the kind of person processing 100+ emails a day who's tired of being their own project manager. If you have one job and one inbox, you probably don't need this yet.
