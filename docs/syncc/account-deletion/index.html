Delete Your Syncc Account

You can permanently delete your Syncc account and all associated data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open Syncc > Profile > Data Controls > Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your profile, people, plans, reminders, check-ins, and outcome history are removed from our systems.

By email

Email richducat@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Syncc account". We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Syncc Plus subscription — cancel it in iOS Settings > Subscriptions.
