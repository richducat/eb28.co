Delete Syncc Data

Syncc does not require an account or sign-in. You can erase the data the app stores on your device at any time.

Erase data on your device

Open Syncc > Settings > Erase All Data. This permanently removes the data Syncc stores on your device, including your profile, saved people, plans, reminders, check-ins, history, and widget data.

Optional AI coach

If you enabled the coach, the data described in our Privacy Policy is sent to our server and forwarded to OpenAI to generate replies. You can withdraw permission in Settings.

For privacy questions or a request about data shared through the coach, email richducat@gmail.com with the subject "Syncc privacy request".

Subscriptions

Erasing your data does not cancel an active Syncc Plus subscription. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
