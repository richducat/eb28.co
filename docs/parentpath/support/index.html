Will my court accept this certificate?

Ask your clerk of court first. ParentPath covers the topic areas Florida requires (Fla. Stat. § 61.21), but it is not currently an approved provider, and each circuit maintains its own accepted-provider list. If your court requires an approved provider, use one from their list.

Why can't I take the quiz yet?

Each module unlocks its quiz after you have read all of its lessons and spent the required 40 minutes reading that module. The timer only runs while a lesson is open on screen, which is what makes the recorded time meaningful. The module screen always shows how much time remains.

Does the reading timer run in the background?

No. It pauses when you leave a lesson, switch apps, or lock your phone, and a single reading burst is capped so an idle screen cannot inflate your total.

I failed a quiz. Can I retake it?

Yes, as many times as you need. You need 80% to pass a module.

How do I get my certificate?

Finish all six modules. The Certificate tab then produces a PDF with your name, case number, county, verified hours, completion date, and a certificate ID. Use the share button to email it, save it to Files, or AirDrop it to your attorney.

I typed my name wrong. Can I fix it?

Settings › Your Court Record. Edit your name, case number, county, or email at any time. If you have already earned your certificate, reset progress to re-issue it with corrected details.

Is my information private?

Completely. There is no account and no server — everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

What is the Practice Lab?

A safe place to rehearse hard messages. A simulated co-parent sends a realistic provocation, you draft a reply, and the app scores it against the BIFF standard taught in Module 2 — Brief, Informative, Friendly, Firm. Nothing you type there is sent to anyone.

I lost my progress after reinstalling.

Course data lives only on the device, so deleting the app removes it. Export your progress from Settings before reinstalling, and keep a copy of your certificate PDF.
