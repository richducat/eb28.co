Owner-review FAQ

What this concept does—and deliberately does not do.

Can this site confirm an appointment?

No. The production site can submit a request to a verified clinic inbox, but the clinic must confirm every appointment.

Does the website provide urgent medical advice?

No. Owner-approved urgent guidance will explain the correct contact boundary without diagnosing or promising availability.

Why is every veterinary content item approved separately?

Clinical and service details can be sensitive. Item-level approval keeps the clinic in control indefinitely.

Will Spanish content be included?

Only if the owner confirms the service and approves a professionally reviewed translation.
