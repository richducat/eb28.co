Public-source results

Search results may be incomplete, outdated, incorrect, or about a different person. Results are leads for human review, not verified facts, proof of identity, or a substitute for official records.

Face Match results

Face Match compares visual features in two guardian-selected photos on the device. A similarity score is not proof that two photos show the same person and must not be treated as a sole basis for accusation, confrontation, emergency action, or a legal conclusion.

No emergency service

The service does not replace emergency responders or law enforcement. Contact local emergency services when a person faces immediate danger.
