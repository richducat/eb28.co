No. Customer calls, forms and appointment actions are intentionally disabled until the owner approves the site and routing is verified.

Are the prices and offers current?

No price or promotion is shown unless the owner confirms it for launch.

What happens after owner approval?

EB28 stages the approved version privately, verifies contact routing, prepares redirects and publishes only after the domain and launch checklist are ready.

The customer keeps the domain, customer data and approved content. ChatbotBuilder retains its reusable software and templates; a static export and domain handoff are available on cancellation.
