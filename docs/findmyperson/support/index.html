Support

Help with guardian accounts, protected profiles, on-device Face Match, missing-person cases, public-source searches, evidence, and account deletion.

Get help

Email support@eb28.co

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, private evidence, or sensitive information about a child.

Face Match help

Face Match requires guardian consent on the protected profile and two photos that each contain one clear face. Apple Vision compares the photos on the iPhone or iPad. The photos are not uploaded or saved to case evidence.

A similarity score is a review aid, not proof of identity, safety, location, or wrongdoing. Confirm every possible match with independent evidence.

Delete an account

Sign in, open the main command center, select Delete account, and confirm permanent deletion. This removes the account and associated profiles, cases, searches, evidence, alerts, watchlist records, and related account data from the service, except information that must be retained by law.

Safety notice

Find My Person is not an emergency service and does not replace law enforcement, emergency responders, or official missing-person resources. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services now.
