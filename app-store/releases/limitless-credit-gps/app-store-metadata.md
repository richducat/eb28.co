## Description

Know the likely credit impact before you make the move.

Limitless Credit GPS is a free credit education and decision simulator. Answer a 60-second quiz — no account, no SSN, no credit pull — and get a personalized plan with your estimated risk level and next best move.

What you can do:

- Simulate 11 common credit moves before you act: pay off a card, open or close a card, request a limit increase, pay a collection, consolidate debt, apply for a mortgage, and more
- See likely direction, confidence, risk, and timing for each move in plain English — never fake point predictions
- Understand sudden score changes with the Why Did My Score Drop explainer
- Follow a step-by-step action plan matched to your goal: buy a home, buy a car, rebuild credit, fix collections, build from scratch, get better rates, business funding, or apartment approval
- Learn the fundamentals in 15 two-minute lessons written in plain English
- Save simulations to your plan and track progress

Private by design: everything you enter stays on your device. No registration, no ads, no tracking.

Limitless Credit GPS provides educational estimates based on common credit scoring factors. It does not guarantee score changes, credit approvals, account removals, or financial outcomes, and it does not provide financial, credit repair, or legal advice.
