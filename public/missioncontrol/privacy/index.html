EB28 Mission Control Privacy

This page describes the EB28 Mission Control iPhone companion and its paired Mac software. Information and service connections depend on how the Mac is configured.

Pairing and network access

The iPhone app stores the Mac name, connection addresses, pairing token and certificate fingerprint in the device's Keychain with a device-only accessibility setting. Requests to the paired Mac use HTTPS, a pairing token and certificate fingerprint checks. Pairing grants access to supported Mac operations; keep the pairing code private.

Information displayed and sent

The phone retrieves task and agent status, questions, replies, usage summaries, daily plans, calendar events, chief-of-staff chat, trading status and the configured TYFYS pipeline from the Mac. Those records may include personal or business information. The app sends actions you choose, such as task edits, agent replies, decisions and chief-of-staff messages, to the paired Mac.

Displayed data is held in the app's current session. The pairing credential is retained in Keychain between launches. Unpairing clears the saved pairing entry and current session views; it does not remove records stored on the Mac or in connected services.

Camera, local network and badges

The app requests camera access to scan pairing QR codes and local network access to connect to the Mac. The scanner reads the pairing code; this flow does not upload camera recordings to a hosted image service. The app may request badge permission to show how many tasks need attention. Manage these permissions in iPhone Settings.

Mac storage and connected services

The Mac software stores configuration and working records in its local Mission Control data folder, including tasks, logs and chief-of-staff chat. Calendar integration reads configured Google calendars through the Mac's selected authenticated profile. Chief-of-staff requests are handed to the configured Hermes profile; its selected model and tools may use local or external services. Other configured agents and integrations can contact their providers and process information under their own settings and policies.

The companion's connection to your Mac does not mean all processing stays on the device. Review the Mac's profiles, agent tools and connected accounts before sending sensitive information. This page does not promise that third-party services retain no data or receive no information.

Your controls and retention

Unpair this phone in More to remove its saved pairing credential and clear current views. Generate a new code or disable phone access on the Mac to revoke phone connections. Delete or manage Mac-side records and service-side records separately; unpairing is not an account or cloud-data deletion operation. Storage and retention in configured services depend on those services and the Mac's configuration.
