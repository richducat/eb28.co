Privacy Policy

Effective July 18, 2026

Syncc ("the app") is operated by DIGITAL SYNC, LLC. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Account details you provide (name, email address, optional phone number), profile details used for planning (such as birth date and a planning city), the people, plans, reminders, check-ins, and outcomes you save, optional photos you attach, and a user ID we generate for your account.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to build your planning views, sync your saved content to your account, send reminders you set, and provide support. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for third-party advertising, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers that host our infrastructure, and only as needed to operate the app. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your controls

You can export your data, correct it, or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app under Profile > Data Controls, or by emailing richducat@gmail.com. See Account Deletion.

Children

Syncc is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
