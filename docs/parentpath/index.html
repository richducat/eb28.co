The divorce parenting course, rebuilt for your phone.

Most Florida parents are ordered to complete a four-hour parent education course before their divorce is final. ParentPath is that course — six modules, real quizzes, honest engagement tracking, and a certificate you can hand the court.

Six required topic areas

Emotional impact, co-parenting, family finances, Florida law, domestic violence and child safety, and conflict resolution — the topic areas of Fla. Stat. § 61.21.

Verified engagement time

The clock runs only while a lesson is open on screen, and pauses when you leave. Four hours means four real hours.

A quiz after every module

80% to pass, unlimited retakes, and quizzes unlock only after the lessons and the time requirement are met.

Practice Lab

Rehearse the hardest texts before you send them. A simulated co-parent provokes; you reply; the app coaches you against the BIFF standard.

A certificate for the court

A PDF with your name, case number, county, verified hours, date, and certificate ID — shareable straight to your attorney.

Private by design

No account. No server. No tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

  1. Enter your name, case number, and county.
