Sec 01 — The problem

The inspection is the easy part. The paperwork is the job.

The re-typing tax

Parcel from the appraiser site. Flood zone from FEMA. Permit date from the county portal. Then you type it all again into the form — every property, every time — and one transposed digit gets the report kicked back by the carrier.

Sixty photos, eight that matter

The carrier needs the panel label, the HVAC data plate, the opening-protection shot. They're buried in a camera roll of sixty frames, and you find the missing one at your desk — after you've left the house.

The 9 p.m. form session

Findings in one app, the 4-Point PDF in another. So you copy, paste, squint at a flattened form, and hope no required field slipped through before it hits underwriting.
