What ParentPath is

A self-paced educational course built around the topic areas of Florida's Parent Education and Family Stabilization Course (Fla. Stat. § 61.21). Its six modules cover the emotional impact of divorce (including children with special needs or existing emotional concerns), co-parenting, family relationships and finances, Florida law affecting children, domestic violence and child safety, and conflict resolution. The statute is the authority on what your circuit requires; read it or ask your clerk rather than relying on this summary. It also includes a practice communication exercise and issues a completion certificate recording your verified engagement time.
