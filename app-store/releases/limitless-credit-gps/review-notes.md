# App Review Notes — Limitless Credit GPS 1.0.0 (build 2)

No login, no account, no payment. The full experience is available immediately on first launch.

## What the app is

A self-contained credit education and decision simulator. All content and simulation
logic run on-device; user answers are stored locally and never uploaded.

## How to reach the core flow

1. Launch the app → welcome screen.
2. Tap **Start My Credit GPS**, pick a goal (e.g., Buy a Home).
3. Answer the 11-question profile quiz (about 60 seconds — ranges and estimates, no SSN,
   no credit pull).
4. The app builds a personalized plan: estimated risk level, next best move, and action items.
5. From the dashboard, **Run a Credit Move** simulates 11 common credit decisions
   (pay off card, open/close card, limit increase, pay collection, consolidate debt,
   apply for mortgage, missed payment) and returns likely direction, confidence, risk,
   and timing with plain-English explanations. The app deliberately never shows fake
   point predictions.
6. **Why Did My Score Drop** matches recent events to common causes. **Learn** contains
   15 original educational lessons. **My Plan** tracks action items and saved simulations.

## Compliance notes

- The app does not access credit reports, does not connect to credit bureaus, and does
  not provide credit repair services. Educational disclaimers appear on the welcome
  screen, quiz, simulation results, and footer.
