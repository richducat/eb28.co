Syncc

Better timing with your people

Syncc is a relationship timing and conversation-planning app for iPhone and iPad. Pick a person, pick the moment: Syncc helps you plan conversations, dates, and big asks at the times they work best — then tracks how they actually went.

What you can do

Keep a private roster of the people who matter, compare candidate days for any conversation or plan, save the plan with a reminder, and log outcomes so every relationship gets your best timing. Syncc Plus expands the system with more saved people, deeper per-relationship workspaces, and longer-range planning views.
