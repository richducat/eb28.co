Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026.

Information the service handles

  • Guardian account information, including name, email address, phone number, and authentication data.
  • Protected-person profiles supplied by the guardian, such as names, aliases, locations, school or organization details, notes, and consent indicators.
  • Missing-person case details, public-source search queries and results, saved evidence, alerts, exports, and case activity.
  • Optional Face Match review metadata, such as a similarity score and classification, when a guardian chooses to save the result to a case.
  • Security, abuse-prevention, and audit information needed to protect accounts and enforce search limits.

How information is used

Information is used to provide guardian-controlled search and case-management features, authenticate accounts, generate requested evidence packages, prevent abuse, maintain service security, and respond to support or legal requests.

The current app does not use advertising tracking and does not sell personal information.

On-device Face Match

With guardian consent, the app can use Apple Vision to detect and compare faces in two photos selected from the device. The comparison runs on the iPhone or iPad. The selected photos and facial feature prints are not uploaded to the service or retained in case evidence.

If the guardian chooses to save the review result, the service stores only result metadata, including the similarity score, classification, comparison method, and confirmation that the photos were not retained. A result cannot establish identity.

Public-source information

Search results may contain information obtained from publicly accessible sources. A result is not proof of identity, wrongdoing, safety, or current location. Guardians are responsible for verifying information and using it lawfully.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the app, disclosed when directed by the account holder, or disclosed when required by law. Account data is retained while the account is active and is deleted when the account is permanently deleted, except where retention is legally required.

Children

The app is designed for parents, guardians, and authorized adults. It is not directed to children and does not invite children to create accounts.

Your choices

You can delete your account from inside the app. Additional access, correction, and deletion instructions are available on the Privacy Choices page.
